FirstProofBench Problem 1 (computability theory)

We consider infinite countable first-order structures, their automorphism groups, and the descriptive
aspects of their representations. A representation A of such a structure is an instance of its
isomorphism type in which the domain is the set of natural numbers N, and the constants, functions,
and relations are elements of N, multi-variable functions from N to N, and multi-place relations
on N, respectively. An automorphism of A, or an isomorphism from A to B, is a bijection of N
preserving the evaluation of all constants, functions, and relations. We let AUT(A) denote the
automorphism group of A.

Definition.
- A is computably AUT-countable if every automorphism of A is computable relative to A.
- A is computably AUT-countable on a cone if there is a C ⊆ N such that whenever B is
  isomorphic to A, every automorphism of B is computable relative to B ⊕ C.

Definition. A function f : A → A is Σ^in_1-definable in A relative to parameters ā = (a_1, . . . , a_n)
from A if there is a family of existential formulas (φ_i)_{i∈N} such that for all x, y ∈ A, f(x) = y if and
only if there is an i ∈ N with A ⊨ φ_i(ā, x, y).

Problem. Is there a countable computably-represented structure A such that A is computably AUT-
countable on a cone, and such that for any finite set of parameters ā in A there is an automorphism
π of A which is not Σ^in_1-definable in A relative to the parameters ā ∪ π(ā), where π(ā) is the image
of ā under π?
