FirstProofBench Problem 8 (matroids and tropical geometry)

Let M be a matroid, and let Trop(M) be the corresponding tropical linear space, i.e. the closure
of the Bergman fan of M in tropical projective space. The relative Dressian of M is

    Dr(M) := {tropical linear subspaces of Trop(M)},

which is partially ordered by inclusion of tropical linear spaces. Suppose M admits an order-
reversing involution F ↦ F^⊥ on the lattice of flats L(M). Note that there is a natural inclusion
L(M)^op ⊂ Dr(M) given by F ↦ Trop(M/F ⊕ U_{0,F}).

Problem. Is there an order-reversing involution Φ on Dr(M) that extends the involution F ↦ F^⊥?
