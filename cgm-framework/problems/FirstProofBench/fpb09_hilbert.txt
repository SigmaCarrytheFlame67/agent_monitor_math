FirstProofBench Problem 9 (algebraic combinatorics)

Let m and n be positive integers and let R^{(m)}_n be the coinvariant algebra with m sets of n
variables. The Hilbert series of an m-multigraded algebra A is

    Hilb(A; q_1, . . . , q_m) = Σ_{i_1,...,i_m ≥ 0} dim( A_{(i_1,...,i_m)} ) q_1^{i_1} · · · q_m^{i_m},

where A_{(i_1,...,i_m)} denotes a multigraded component, with grading variables q_1, . . . , q_m. Let
s_λ(q_1, . . . , q_m) denote a Schur polynomial. For R^{(m)}_n, it is known that its Hilbert series may
be expressed by

    Hilb(R^{(m)}_n; q_1, . . . , q_m) = Σ_λ c_λ(n) s_λ(q_1, . . . , q_m),

for some nonnegative universal series coefficients c_λ(n) which do not depend on m.

Problem. For any n, give a combinatorial interpretation of the coefficients c_λ(n) when λ is a hook
shape partition.
