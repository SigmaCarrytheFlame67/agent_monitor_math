FirstProofBench Problem 6 (lattice theory)

Let T denote a finite, weighted tree with vertex set V , edge set E, and weight function w: V → Z.
Let L(T) denote the free abelian group generated by V , equipped with the symmetric bilinear form
· whose value on a pair of generators u, v ∈ V is given by

    u · v = { w(u),  u = v;
            { −1,    (u, v) ∈ E;
            { 0,     u ≠ v, (u, v) ∉ E.

Suppose that the form is positive definite, and suppose that there exists a single vertex v for which
the vertex weight w(v) is less than the vertex degree d(v). Prove that T contains a vertex u such
that u is an irreducible element of L(T), i.e. there do not exist nonzero lattice elements a, b ∈ L(T)
such that u = a + b and a · b ≥ 0.
