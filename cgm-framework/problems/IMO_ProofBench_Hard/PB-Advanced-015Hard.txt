Consider an acute triangle $ABC$ that is not isosceles. Let $H_0$, $E$, and $F$ be the feet of the perpendiculars dropped from vertices $A$, $B$, and $C$ to their opposite sides, respectively. Let $D$ be the point where the incircle of $\triangle ABC$ is tangent to side $ BC $. Denote the incenter and circumcenter of $\triangle ABC$ as $I$ and $O$, respectively. Let $K$ be the intersection of line $IO$ and line $BC$. Let $Q$ be the point where the ray $IH_0$ intersects the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ again. Let $X$ be the point where the line $ QD $ intersects the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ at a point other than $Q$. Let $Y$ be the point where the circle that touches rays $AB$, $AC$, and is also externally tangent to the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$, touches the circumcircle of $ \triangle ABC$. Prove that if segment $EF$ is tangent to the incircle of $ \triangle ABC$, then $X$, $Y$, and $K$ are collinear.
